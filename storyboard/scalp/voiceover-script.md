## Product facts used (retail listings)
- PHYTO PDRN, caffeine, CICA reedles, silk amino acids, propolis extract.
- Twist the brush head to ON, apply to the areas you want, and massage with the brush in the direction your hair grows. Use on towel-dried or dry hair.
- Users report a cooling or tingling feeling.
- Not used: biotin and rosemary (one reference says them, but retail listings don't confirm). No hair-growth claims.
