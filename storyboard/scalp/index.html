02 · brush in the way your hair grows

Yours: keep your glow up look (Jost pills, Pretendard captions on dusty rose) so the series stays recognisable. Borrow two things: the outlined white opening title (Ref B) and lowercase + emoji endings (Ref D).

Captions · Pacing · Techniques

  • Pacing: the references average 1.0–2.7s per shot. The three short ones (16–24s) cut every 1–1.8s. Your plan: 32s, 17 clips, about 1.9s each, with text changing every 1–1.5s so it feels faster.
  • Captions: every 1–1.5s, 2–6 words. Talking videos follow the voice word for word (B). Music videos use 1 caption per idea (A, C, D).
  • Hook in 2s: show the result first (A), or a relatable problem (C).
  • Transitions: push the product into the lens, and a hand swipe for the before→after (A).
  • Satisfying close-ups: the ON twist, serum through the bristles, a close-up on the parting (C, D).
  • Product explained in 2–3 short facts (B, C, D).
  • Ending: a soft "don't sleep on this" / "stay tuned" (B, D). Yours: save + annyeong.
  • Claims: refs B and C promise hair growth and target women "balding right now". Skip that: say "healthy-looking scalp" and "fresh roots" instead.

🎥 Filming checklist
  • Back camera, 4K, 30fps. Use 60fps for shots 6 and 12a.
  • Face a window, or a warm lamp in front of you. No overhead-only light.
  • Plain background. White or cream robe + towel.
  • Phone on a tripod at eye level for shots 1, 4, 8, 10, 14.
  • Macros (2, 5, 6, 9b): tap to focus, phone 10–15cm away.
  • Shot 11: film the swipe twice, once in the robe and once in your outfit.
  • Wipe the teal brush clean between takes.
  • Record the voiceover separately in a quiet room.
